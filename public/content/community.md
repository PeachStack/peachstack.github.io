# Community — Peach Stack | Student Network & Resources

**URL:** https://peachstack.github.io/community  
**Audience:** Current and prospective Peach Stack student interns

---

## Overview

The Peach Stack community is where students connect, share resources, and grow their professional networks. When you join a Peach Stack cohort, you become part of a peer network of ambitious college students from universities across the country.

## What the Community Offers

- Peer-to-peer networking with fellow interns and alumni
- Access to shared resources and learning materials
- Community events and check-ins
- Professional development guidance
- A space to collaborate, share wins, and ask questions

## Values

Peach Stack's community is built on:
- Accountability — showing up and doing the work
- Collaboration — lifting each other as you climb
- Honesty — real feedback, real growth
- Professionalism — conducting yourself like the career you're building

## How to Join

Join the community by applying to a Peach Stack cohort: https://peachstack.github.io/apply
