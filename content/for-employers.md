## Overview

Peach Stack gives businesses access to motivated, pre-vetted college students ready to do real work. We handle the matching, onboarding, and management — you get the output.

## Why Hire Through Peach Stack?

- **Pre-vetted talent** — Students are screened and matched to your needs
- **Managed experience** — We handle cohort coordination and quality control
- **Real deliverables** — Students work on scoped projects with clear outcomes
- **Affordable** — Pay for actual work, not overhead
- **Fast onboarding** — Simple process to get started
- **Atlanta-based, national reach** — Access talent from top universities

## What Students Can Do For You

- Sales prospecting and business development outreach
- Market research and competitive analysis
- Operations and process improvement consulting
- Tech projects: automation, data pipelines, tooling, dashboards
- Marketing: content, social media, growth campaigns, brand assets

## How It Works

1. Submit an employer inquiry at https://peachstack.github.io/for-employers
2. Peach Stack matches you with a curated cohort of students
3. Students are onboarded with your project context
4. You get regular updates and deliverables
5. Pay only for completed, quality work

## Who Uses Peach Stack?

Small to medium businesses, startups, and entrepreneurs in Atlanta and beyond who want junior talent they can trust without spending months training or hiring full-time.
