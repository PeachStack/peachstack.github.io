## Tagline

Turn Zero Experience into a Career Stack.

## What Is Peach Stack?

Peach Stack bridges the gap between students who need experience and businesses that need reliable junior talent. We place motivated college students in structured, project-based internships — with real deliverables, real mentorship, and real career outcomes.

We connect students with vetted employers for high-impact work. Based in Atlanta, building nationally.

## How It Works

### For Students
1. Apply through the student application form
2. Get matched to a cohort and role track (Sales, Consulting, Tech, Marketing)
3. Work on real projects for real clients
4. Build a portfolio and gain verifiable experience
5. Launch your career from day one

### For Employers
1. Submit an employer inquiry
2. Get matched with pre-vetted student talent
3. Simple onboarding — Peach Stack manages the interns
4. Pay for real output, not overhead

## Available Internship Tracks

- **Sales & Business Development** — Identify and connect with small business owners, build relationships, drive client acquisition
- **Consulting, Business Track** — Work with clients on strategy, operations, and cost-effective solutions
- **Consulting, Tech Track** — Build technical solutions, automation, and data pipelines for clients
- **Marketing** — Content, growth, and brand work for real businesses

## Why Peach Stack?

- Real projects, not busywork
- Vetted, managed students — not a job board
- Mentorship built in
- Career outcomes, not just experience hours
