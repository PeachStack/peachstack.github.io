## Overview

Peach Stack is looking for motivated college students ready to do real work, build real skills, and get real results. This is not a simulation. You will work directly with clients, produce deliverables, and walk away with experience that actually shows up on a resume.

## Available Roles

### Sales & Business Development
Identify and connect with small business owners, build relationships, and bring in clients who are ready to grow. For people who are confident, personable, and hungry to learn how real business development works.

### Consulting, Business Track
Work directly with clients to understand their needs and build cost-effective solutions, focused on strategy and operations.

### Consulting, Tech Track
Build technical solutions — automation, data pipelines, tooling — for real clients. For students who can code and want to apply those skills in a business context.

### Marketing
Content creation, growth strategy, and brand work for real companies. For students who understand audiences and can create compelling materials that drive results.

## Who Should Apply?

- College students (any year, any major)
- Self-starters who want real work experience
- Students who are eager to build a portfolio
- Anyone tired of unpaid, busywork internships

## What You Get

- Real project work with real clients
- Mentorship and structured cohort experience
- Portfolio-ready deliverables
- Professional references and verifiable experience
- Community of peers and alumni
